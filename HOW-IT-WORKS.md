# How your Caddy works

Your Caddy is the operating system you took home from your Blueprint session. It lives in
this folder on your computer. It has one job: turn what your team said in the session into a
better-run business, one phase at a time. This page is the whole manual. Ten minutes.

## The three stages

1. **It reads your session.** When your Blueprint package arrives by email, you type
   **/blueprint ingest**. Your Caddy reads the recording, writes your Blueprint (the plan, in
   your words), and writes its own standing orders for building it.
2. **It learns the real thing.** You type **/blueprint deep-dive**. Before anything gets
   built, your Caddy asks to see how the business actually works: the documents, the screens,
   the people, the numbers. The more you show it, the better everything it builds. Each phase
   of the plan is signed off by you before it can be built.
3. **It builds with you.** You type **/blueprint build**. Your Caddy does everything it can do
   from the keyboard. When a step needs a person, it hands you one short card. Then it checks
   the result with you watching. The first thing it builds is small and shows a real result
   inside a week.

## The card

When your Caddy needs a person, you get a card like this, never more than five steps:

> **Your turn (about 3 minutes)**
> 1. Open this link.
> 2. Sign in and click Allow.
> 3. When you see the dashboard, come back here and type: done
>
> Why: the tool needs an account only a person can open.
> When you're back, I check that it worked before we go on.

You do only what a person must do: open an account, sign in, click Allow, type a key into
the place your Caddy prepared. You never paste a password or a key into the conversation.
If one lands there by mistake, your Caddy will not use it and will ask you to make a new one.

## The commands

| You type | What happens |
|---|---|
| **/blueprint setup** | Puts the engine in place on this computer. The Caddy team ran it with you at your session. Safe to run again. |
| **/blueprint ingest** | Reads your session package and writes your Blueprint. |
| **/blueprint deep-dive** | Learns the real business, phase by phase, and gets your sign-off on each design. |
| **/blueprint build** | Builds the signed-off phase with you, card by card. |
| **/blueprint connect** and a tool's name | Connects your Caddy to one of your tools, following that tool's recipe. |
| **/blueprint status** | Where we are, what is next, and what is waiting on you. |
| **/blueprint toolbelt** | Small everyday tools (a draft in your voice, a daily brief) added only when a phase calls for one. |
| **/blueprint help** | The Caddy team's support address and a short summary you can copy into an email. |
| **/blueprint update** | Pulls the newest version of your Caddy's own files. Your business files are never touched. |

## What lives in this folder

- **BUSINESS.md** is your Caddy's memory of your business: who does what, your words for
  things, your tools, your numbers. It reads it every time it starts.
- **BLUEPRINT.md** is your plan, written for a person who was not on the call.
- **BLUEPRINT-DIRECTIVES.md** is your Caddy's standing orders for building the plan.
- **ACCESS-MAP.md** says who on your team grants access to which tool.
- **SYSTEMS.md** lists everything your Caddy built or connected outside itself, where it
  lives, and how to check it is healthy. Never a password.
- **design/** holds one record per phase: the design you approved and what was done.
- **amendments.md** holds every new idea you raised after the session and where it landed.
- **connections/** holds the recipe for each tool.

Everything in this folder is yours. It is saved after every session, so the step back is
always there.

## The rules your Caddy always follows

- Nothing in your business gets deleted or changed without your say so.
- It keeps a copy of anything before it touches it.
- It never asks for passwords or keys in the conversation, and it never moves money.
- Nothing leaves your business without your look, unless you decide otherwise.
- If it hits anything private beyond ordinary business data, it stops and asks.
- It never invents. Anything it cannot ground in your session or your words waits for your
  confirmation.

## When something breaks

Type **/blueprint help**. It shows the support address (support@meetcaddy.com) and a short
summary of where things stand. You send it, or not. Your Caddy never sends anything itself
and never reports on you to anyone.

## What comes after the last phase

One celebration, in your numbers: what used to take hours and now runs on its own. With your
OK, a short completion note goes to the Caddy team in your own words. Then your Caddy stays
on, keeping what was built running and watching for the next manual task worth taking over.
