# Your Blueprint package lands here

Two files arrive by email from the Caddy team soon after your session:

1. The transcript of your session (`transcript.md` or `transcript.txt`): the written record.
2. The intake document (`intake-doc.md`): the answers you gave when you booked.

Save both into this folder, then type **/blueprint ingest**. That is the whole job.

At your install, the Caddy team may already have dropped `intake-doc.md` and a short
`session-recap.md` in here. That is how your Caddy knows your name, your business and your biggest
time sink before the package arrives. It does not start building from them; it waits for the
transcript, which is the only source of your plan.
