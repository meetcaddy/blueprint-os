# Your Blueprint package lands here

Two files arrive by email from the Caddy team soon after your session, and sometimes a third:

1. The transcript of your session (`transcript.md` or `transcript.txt`): the written record.
2. The intake document (`intake-doc.md`): the answers you gave when you booked, and your
   Audit Workbook when you filled one in.
3. Sometimes, the Caddy team's notes for your Caddy (`roadmap-brief.md`): what the team heard in
   your session and checked afterwards, written for your Caddy to read. It adds to the
   transcript and never replaces it. If your package has no such file, nothing is missing.

Save what arrived into this folder, then type **/blueprint ingest**. That is the whole job. The
transcript is what your plan is built from: the notes alone are not enough, and your Caddy will
say so and wait for the transcript.

At your install, the Caddy team may already have dropped `intake-doc.md` and a short
`session-recap.md` in here. That is how your Caddy knows your name, your business and your biggest
time sink before the package arrives. It does not start building from them; it waits for the
transcript, which is the only source of your plan.
