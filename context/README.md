# Your drop folder

Drop anything about your business here, and your Caddy reads it before it asks you a question.
The more you drop, the less you get asked. The files themselves never go into your saved copy.
Only a short list of what is here, and what your Caddy learned from it, gets saved. When your
Caddy opens a file, it reads it through your own AI plan, the same as anything you type here.

## The slots

| Folder | Drop here |
|---|---|
| `how-we-work/` | SOPs, checklists, process documents, "how we do it" notes, the forms you use |
| `people/` | the org chart, who does what, job descriptions, who covers what |
| `money/` | the P&L, your pricing, estimates, invoices, quotes, the chart of accounts |
| `customers/` | a few real jobs from start to finish, sample quotes, what a customer record looks like |
| `tools/` | screenshots of your software, exports, the reports your systems produce |
| `recordings/` | videos, voice memos, screen recordings of how a job is done |
| `anything-else/` | whatever does not fit above |

Each folder has a short note with ideas of what helps most. Folders inside folders are fine.

## Keep these out

- Passwords, keys, card numbers, bank log-ins. Your Caddy tries to spot a file that looks like one
  (by its name, or by a line inside it), names it, asks you to remove it, and does not use it.
  It cannot catch everything, and it does not look inside PDFs or pictures for them, so keep them
  out all the same. If it names a file that is fine (an SOP about resetting passwords, say), tell
  it so and it reads the file.
- Anyone's medical records.
- Employee ID numbers, Social Security numbers, personal bank details.

Ordinary business documents are exactly what belongs here. If something is private beyond
ordinary business data, your Caddy pauses and asks before using it.

## Then

Once your Blueprint is built, type **/blueprint context**. Your Caddy reads what is new, writes
`INDEX.md` (what is here, what it learned, what would help next) and uses it in every design from
then on. Drop more any time. Before the Blueprint is built it only lists what is here.

Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), PDF, PNG and JPG pictures, text files and
spreadsheets (.csv) are read directly. Older Office files (.doc reads; .xls and .ppt do not),
Pages, Numbers and Keynote: export them as PDF and drop that. Recordings need a transcript: your
Caddy offers the easiest way once, and never installs anything on its own.
